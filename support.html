Frequently asked questions

I bought the app on another device — how do I get it back?
Open the app's settings and tap "Restore Purchases". Anything bought with the same Apple ID or Google account is restored.
The app is not working correctly. What should I do?
Close and reopen the app, and make sure you have the latest version installed. If the problem persists, email us a short description of what you did and what happened.
How do I delete my data?
Your data is stored on your device. Delete an individual entry in the app, or remove the app to delete all of it.
Is my data secure?
Your data stays in the app's protected data area on your device. The app has no server component, so nothing is uploaded to us.
